# The Awesome Adventures of Bubbles

### Chapter Two
Now, you should know, that Bubbles is no ordinary octopus. Oh no no no, Bubbles is a very special octopus, a very special octopus indeed.

When Bubbles is happy, she is pink, and the cuteness of her smile lights up the entire coral reef. She is a little pink bulb bobbing in the water, her dainty tentacles fluttering gently like the dress of a dancer.

But when Bubbles is annoyed, she turns blue: a dazzling, bright sky blue that washes the oceans cerulean with her emotion. Yet even when angry, the adorable pout on her face fills you with irresistible laughter, and the inexplicable urge to hug her until she smiles again.

And indeed, squishing Bubbles is the perfect way to make her happy again.

Bubbles is quite unlike other octopi. While their tentacles are longer, and thicker, squared off and detached, like the petals of a flower, Bubbles’s tentacles are small and pointy, perhaps like a... a rose, or a pie. Yeah, a cutiepie. And never forget that there is noo other octopus in the world like Bubbles.

She is, simply, one of a kind.
