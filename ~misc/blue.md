**Conjecture**.

Let $G(A)$ denote the closed set of female characters in a series $A$, defined only if there is at least one $g \in G(A)$ such that $g$ has blue hair.

We postulate that there is a *strong positive correlation* between:

- $[P]$: $g$ is best girl.
- $[Q]$: $g$ has blue hair.

Intuitively, we claim that $P$ strongly implies $Q$, and vice versa: “most best girls have blue hair, and blue hair girls are often best girl”.


<br>


## Definitions

- We use ***series*** to collectively refer to any film, animation, anime, manga, webcomic or similar visual media.
- $P$ states that in the partial order of favouritism on $G(A)$, $g$ is the maximal element.
- $Q$ includes any shade of blue, but overwhelmingly media use darker shades of blue for hair.


<br>


## Empirical Evidence

### Proofs
We identify the following series to satisfy the conjecture:

- *Oshi no Ko*: **Akane Kurokawa**, closely tailed by Ruby Hoshino.
- *Domestic Girlfriend*: **Rui Tachibana** clears.
- *Close As Neighbours*: **May** and it’s not even close.
- *Arcane*: **Caitlyn** and Vi would share, but young Caitlyn is way cuter than Vi and gives her the victory.
- *Shikimori-san’s Not Just A Cutie*: **Kamiya** is on equal footing with Shikimori.

> (In descending order of “how strongly I feel she is best girl”)

### Counterexamples
However, the following series violate the conjecture:

- *Yu-Gi-Oh: ARC-V*: **Sora** is disgusting, and there are indeed no other blue-haired girls 💔
- *Kanojo, Okarishimasu*: **Ruka**, no thanks lmao
