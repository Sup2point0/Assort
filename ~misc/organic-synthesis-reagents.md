## Oxidation

**K<sub>2</sub>Cr<sub>2</sub>O<sub>7</sub>** (*potassium dichromate*) oxidises alcohols to ketones, or if they’re primary, to aldehydes. Heat under reflux to further oxidise into carboxylic acids.


<br>


## Reduction

### Reduction
**NaBrH<sub>4</sub>** (*sodium borohydride*) is a mild reducing agent.

**LiAlH<sub>4</sub>** (*lithium aluminium hydride*) is a much more potent reducing agent that reduces ketones and carboxylic acids right down to alcohols.

### Elimination
**KOH** in hot **C<sub>2</sub>H<sub>5</sub>OH** eliminates **HX** from halogenated compounds to form an alkene.


<br>

### Chlorination
