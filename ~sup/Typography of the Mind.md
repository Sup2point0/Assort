# Typography of the Mind

Roman is rational, but rich in emotion and personality. They’ve been accused of main-character syndrome, but this is mostly due to their generic appearance.

**Bold** is matter-of-fact. They don’t sugar-coat things, and will state things as they are. Sometimes it’s the harsh truth. They don’t speak often, but when they do it’s with weight and conviction.

*Italic* is lively, unpredictable, and ironic. They can be lighthearted and inventive, often brightening the conversation, but can also be ruthlessly biting, emphasising their statements with undiluted cruelty.

*Oblique* is floaty and eloquent, although they often contribute very little to discussion. They follow the wind, and delight in the confusion of others. They don’t see why we need to care so much.

__Underline__ sometimes appears to be taking after others, but more often seeks to cause discord and distress. They voice the unspoken thoughts that others don’t venture into. They pester and pickle, but keep us in check.

~~Strikethrough~~ is rogue. They do what they want, and say what they want. Sometimes they’re joking, other times it’s less clear. They like playing devil’s advocate – in fact, it’s one of the things they’re best at.

Humanist is real but personal. They hold their values, emotions and memories close. They usually engage in discussion and (non-heated) debate with Roman.

Harmony seeks to maintain balance. They’re disinclined towards extreme or intense opinions, instead choosing to retain a cool and collected approach. They often come across as serene and utopic.

Kerning lives by feeling. Numbers and details are inferior for them; it is the direction and vibe that matters for them. 

Ligature sleeps most of the time. They occasionally pop up to catch up with everyone. They’re good friends with Oblique.

Void is silent. They’re there, biding their time. Darkness enshrouds them.
