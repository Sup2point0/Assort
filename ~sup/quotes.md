## *Suffering builds character.*

A bit morbid, but it’s true.

Being challenged grows your resilience and motivation. Struggling on learning or solving problems makes you think. Failure teaches you more than success.

Boredom and isolation teach you to entertain yourself. Restriction and loss help you appreciate the things you do have. Suffering at the hands of others teaches you to never inflict that same harm upon anyone else.[^harm]

[^harm]: Uh, hopefully. At least that’s how it was with me 0.o

Of course, this can come with costs such as trauma and mental health damage, so...
